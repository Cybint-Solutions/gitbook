# Settings

## Campaign Settings

In this section, you can specify the campaign name, associated client, and additional automation options.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (7) (1) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Name and Client

Every campaign requires a name and an associated client.\
These settings are displayed in the [campaign dashboard](../../../campaigns-dashboards.md).

#### Stop Date

If enabled, the campaign will automatically stop at this time. You can also choose to automatically send a campaign report to the campaign administrator once the campaign ends.

#### Industry Benchmark

If you want to compare your phishing results with companies in the same industry sector, you can enable benchmarking. If selected, your **anonymized** results will be included in the benchmarking tool, sharing only overall success rates **without any user- or client-specific data**.

For more on benchmarking read [here](../../../settings/benchmark-sectors.md).
